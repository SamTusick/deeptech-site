The rocket

About twelve feet tall, flying on an L-class motor.

This season's vehicle is a high-power rocket built by the Rocket Building Team. It will also carry the two student payloads selected in the Payload Challenge.

Performance figures are predictions and will be updated as the design is finalized.

Fall 2026 · Payload Challenge

Two student payloads. One real rocket flight.

FGCU students from any major design a small experiment that tests a technology with a real-world application. Two teams are selected, each receives $500 for payload components, and both experiments fly on our rocket.
